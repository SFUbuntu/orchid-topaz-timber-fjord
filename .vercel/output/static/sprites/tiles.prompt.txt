16-bit Super Nintendo top-down terrain tileset for VECTOR FANG.
Seamless fill plates (desert sand, mountain rock, ocean water, grass, packed dirt)
generated then SNES 15-bit quantized, Bayer-dithered, and sliced to 16×16.
Mountain slope / peak / ridge / cliff tiles painted with directional light.
Water uses 4-frame horizontal wavelet animation. Shores include N/S/E/W foam.
